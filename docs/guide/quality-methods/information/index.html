The two item parameters

b — Difficulty
logit(1 − solve_rate); harder tasks (low solve-rate) get higher b.
a — Discrimination
max(0.2, |r_pb|·2 + 0.2), where r_pb is the task’s point-biserial correlation with the per-model total score. An all-pass, all-fail or non-correlating item floors to a small a — it discriminates nothing.

KR-20 — Reliability

Kuder-Richardson 20 — internal-consistency reliability for binary items.

Reading KR-20

k, p_i, q_i, σ²_total
Tasks; the pass rate of task i; 1 − p_i; the per-model total-score variance.
Undefined
None when k < 2, or when that variance is 0.
Rule of thumb
> 0.7 acceptable, > 0.8 good; a negative value means the items are mutually inconsistent.
