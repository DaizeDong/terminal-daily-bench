Scope and limits

Keep the conclusion within what the evaluated tasks and configurations can support.

What the summary describes

Quality measures describe the observed task set and evaluated models. They can reveal poor separation, narrow difficulty coverage, or inconsistent outcomes. They do not change task scores or measure how often protected tests accept an incorrect solution.

Model and agent effects

A result reflects both the model and the agent configuration around it. Changing tools, prompts, or interaction limits can change performance. Compare models under the same setup, or describe the result as a comparison of complete configurations.

Limits on conclusions

  • Small task sets give coarse estimates and wide uncertainty intervals.
  • High consistency is uninformative when the models are too similar to separate.
  • Low reliability limits how confidently a total score can represent one ability.
  • Projected task counts assume new tasks resemble those already observed.

Quality summaries and readiness assessments are guidance for interpretation, not certification.
