Information and reliability

Estimate how precisely and consistently a task set separates the evaluated models.

Information

The information estimate combines how difficult a task is with how well its outcomes distinguish models. Tasks that are too easy or too hard for the evaluated models usually contribute little. More information can support more precise comparisons under the model’s assumptions.

How it is estimated

Task difficulty and discrimination are approximated from observed success rates and their relationship to total scores. The resulting estimate depends on this group of models and tasks; it is not an intrinsic property of a task.

Reliability

KR-20 measures internal consistency for tasks with pass-or-fail outcomes. It asks whether the tasks support a reasonably consistent total score across the evaluated models.

Read reliability with care

Reliability is unavailable with fewer than two tasks or when all models have the same total score. A negative value indicates inconsistent task outcomes. Values around 0.7 or higher are a common rule of thumb, not a guarantee that the benchmark measures one general ability.

See Scope and limits before using a total score as a skill estimate.
